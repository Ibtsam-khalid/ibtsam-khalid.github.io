About

I trained in Pakistan, where I spent five years at the University of Lahore and its teaching hospital. The hospital runs around the clock, so I got experience in A&E, trauma, ICU mobiles and outpatient lists. After a year qualified there, I came to the UK for an MSc in Healthcare Management. I wanted to understand how the NHS works as a whole organisation, not just how its imaging departments run.

Since then I've gained HCPC registration and arranged my own observership at Maidstone and Tunbridge Wells NHS Trust. I now work as a bank MRI radiographer. My aim is to specialise in MRI and, in the longer term, train as a reporting radiographer.

I love this work. Helping patients through a scan, especially ones who are frightened or in pain, is the reason I chose radiography.
